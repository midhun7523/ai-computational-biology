# AI & Computational Biology: Curriculum Map

This is a high-level guide to how the subjects in the supplied SSSIHL syllabus can connect. It is not a substitute for the official course outline.

## Semester-by-semester view

### Semester 1 — Foundations
The supplied syllabus includes Differential Calculus, Introduction to Algorithms, The Dynamic Cell, Introduction to Laboratory Science and Basic Programming in Python, alongside language, awareness and multidisciplinary courses.

**Why it matters:** calculus introduces quantitative change; algorithms and Python build computational thinking; cell biology and laboratory science introduce biological systems and evidence.

### Semester 2 — Programming and microorganisms
The supplied syllabus includes Integral Calculus, Discrete Mathematics, Microbiology, practical work in Cell Biology and Microbiology, and Programming, Data Structures and Algorithms using Python.

**Why it matters:** students begin connecting biological concepts to structured computational problem-solving.

### Semester 3 — Quantitative methods and molecular biology
The supplied PDF lists Linear Algebra, Introduction to Statistics, Biochemistry and a practical course in Biochemistry, plus Data Visualization with R and general/awareness/multidisciplinary courses.

**Student-provided curriculum update:** Molecular Biology is taught instead of Biochemistry in Semester 3. This repository uses Molecular Biology as the updated title, but the uploaded PDF has not been revised to reflect that change.

**Why it matters:** linear algebra and statistics underpin many data-science methods, R supports data visualisation, and molecular biology helps explain the information flow and mechanisms that generate biological data.

### Semester 4 — Modelling foundations
The supplied syllabus includes Differential Equations, Design and Analysis of Algorithms, Principles of Genetics, Probability Theory and Distributions, and an introductory AI skill course.

**Why it matters:** probability, algorithms, genetics and AI create a bridge between biological questions and quantitative models.

### Semester 5 — Molecular and computational biology
The supplied syllabus includes Molecular Biology and its practical course, Statistical Inference, Bioinformatics and Computational Biology, a specialisation elective, and Cyber Security.

**Why it matters:** this is a direct meeting point for biological knowledge, statistical reasoning and computational analysis.

### Semester 6 — Applying AI to biology
The supplied syllabus includes Database Management Systems, Artificial Intelligence for Computational Biology 1, Mathematics for Machine Learning, Epidemiology and Public Health, a specialisation elective, and a mini-project.

**Why it matters:** students encounter data storage, machine-learning foundations, population-level health questions and project work.

### Semester 7 — Advanced topics
The supplied syllabus includes Multi-OMICS, Machine Learning and AI, specialisation electives and project/research components. The honours and honours-with-research pathways have different research-related requirements.

**Why it matters:** multi-omics combines different layers of biological measurement, while machine learning can help identify patterns across complex datasets.

### Semester 8 — Modelling and project work
The supplied syllabus includes Mathematical Modelling, a specialisation elective and a project. The honours and honours-with-research routes specify different project structures.

**Why it matters:** modelling and project work provide opportunities to apply knowledge to a focused question and communicate the results.

## Electives listed in the supplied syllabus

- Stem Cell and Regenerative Biology
- Pharmacology and Drug Designing
- Gene Regulation and Expression
- Cancer Biology
- Genetic Engineering
- Neurobiology
- Database Management Systems and SQL Programming
- Artificial Intelligence for Computational Biology 2

Availability and placement of electives may depend on the current department offering.

## The interdisciplinary connection

| Foundation | Enables |
|---|---|
| Mathematics | Quantitative models, optimisation and understanding model behaviour |
| Statistics | Handling uncertainty, inference and evaluating evidence |
| Programming and algorithms | Automating analysis and building reproducible tools |
| Biology and molecular biology | Formulating meaningful biological questions |
| AI and machine learning | Learning patterns and making predictions from data |
| Bioinformatics and multi-omics | Analysing sequences and complex molecular datasets |
| Projects and research | Combining the above in a practical investigation |

## Important note

The uploaded PDF is titled *Syllabus for B.S. (Honours) & B.S. (Honours with Research) in Artificial Intelligence and Computational Biology*, effective from the June 2024 batch onwards. Because course offerings can change, confirm current course titles, credits and assessment details with SSSIHL before relying on this document for official academic decisions.
