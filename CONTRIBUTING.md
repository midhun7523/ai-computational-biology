# Contributing

Contributions are welcome from students and readers who want to make this repository more accurate and useful.

## Good contributions
- Clarifying a course concept in plain language
- Adding a beginner-friendly, reproducible project
- Correcting a broken link or outdated source
- Adding a properly cited research example
- Improving accessibility and readability

## Please follow these principles
1. Cite original sources for factual claims.
2. Separate official syllabus information from student interpretations and external research.
3. Do not promise jobs, salaries or immunity from AI automation.
4. Do not upload private health data, confidential research or copyrighted material without permission.
5. Explain limitations and assumptions in project results.
6. Keep explanations respectful, clear and useful for beginners.

## Reporting corrections
Open an issue describing the page, the proposed correction and a supporting source. For syllabus changes, prefer the latest official department-issued document.
